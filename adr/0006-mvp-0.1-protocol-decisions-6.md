# ADR 0006: MVP 0.1 protocol decisions, sixth batch

- **Status:** accepted, per item (see the Status column)
- **Decided by:** the orchestrator while the project owner is away (owner
  AFK), pending owner review. The owner asked the orchestrator to proceed
  autonomously.
- **Applied by:** SPEC-PATCH-07 (baseline `mvp-0.1-baseline.7`)

## Context

The pre-release security review (`.github: docs/release/security-review-mvp-0.1.md`)
found two decisions it could not settle without the specification.

**H1:** Automerge's columnar format lets a small chunk expand without
bound, and both SDKs handed received chunks to their engine before any
size check.
- A compressed change chunk, or a Snapshot's deflated columns, inflate
  without a cap. A 255 KiB chunk took 1.36 GiB of RSS.
- Run-length encoding alone does the same in an uncompressed change. A
  112-byte change built by Automerge's own encoder expanded to 1,000,000
  operations and 1.2 GiB in Automerge JS 3.5.0.
- A writer only needs `data/write`; a Snapshot publisher needs
  `snapshot/publish`.

**M1:** a client adopted the server's advertised maximum message size with
no upper bound.

The SDK fixes also needed a bound on the nesting depth of values (M7). The
two SDKs must agree on it, because it decides whether an object is
profile-valid.

## Decisions

| ID | Status | Decision | Sections changed | Vectors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CHANGE-LIMITS | Orchestrator decision (owner AFK), pending owner review | A change has EXACT limits, enforced before the engine: 16,384 values per column (columns holding a group's entries are bounded by the group instead); predecessors per operation at most one plus the change's other actors (an operation's predecessors are concurrent, so they have distinct actors), 262,144 in total; 4 MiB of expanded strings; 1,024 dependencies and other actors. They are counted from the chunk structure and the run headers alone, by the stated rules. Exact, because acceptance decides replica state (§14.1): receivers must agree. Writers MUST NOT exceed them; an oversized transaction fails locally. Over the limits: `PROFILE_INVALID` / `INVALID_AUTOMERGE_BYTES` | SHARED-OBJECTS-PROFILE-01 §11.1, §74.1 | corpus `expansion`: cases at and one past each limit, the measured RLE bomb, a predecessor bomb |
| CHANGE-STRUCTURE | Orchestrator decision (owner AFK), pending owner review | Before the engine: no two columns share a specification, every actor index is in range, and every other actor of a change is already an actor of the document. Any engine failure on an admitted change (an error, or an abort the receiver catches) is `INVALID_AUTOMERGE_BYTES`, and the document is restored. All of these were found by fuzzing the sdk-rs prototype walker: inputs that pass the walk but abort Automerge (JS PanicError, an automerge-rs unwrap) | SHARED-OBJECTS-PROFILE-01 §11.1, §74.1 | `EXP-change-duplicate-column`, `EXP-change-actor-index`, negative `SO-UNKNOWN-ACTOR` |
| CHANGE-UNCOMPRESSED | Orchestrator decision (owner AFK), pending owner review | A Data Unit carries an uncompressed change chunk (type 1) only. A compressed change (type 2) is rejected. Writers frame raw change bytes: automerge-rs `Change::bytes()` compresses above 256 bytes, while Automerge JS never compresses. Every published vector already uses type 1 | SHARED-OBJECTS-PROFILE-01 §11 | `EXP-change-compressed` |
| SNAPSHOT-LIMITS | Orchestrator decision (owner AFK), pending owner review | A Snapshot is one document chunk with nothing after it, checked before the engine with LOCAL limits. Every receiver accepts at least the floor: 262,144 values, 262,144 group entries, 32 MiB of strings, 32 MiB of inflated column data, 1,024 actors and heads. Deflated columns are inflated under a running cap. Local, because Snapshot acceptance does not change state (the receiver falls back to the units) | SHARED-OBJECTS-PROFILE-01 §13, §13.1 | `EXP-snapshot-*` (at and one past the floor, trailing chunk) |
| VALUE-DEPTH | Orchestrator decision (owner AFK), pending owner review | An object's maps and lists nest at most 64 levels (the field's own map is depth 1). Depth 65 is `INVALID_FIELD_TYPE` at its own pointer, and nothing below it is examined. Matches sdk-rs bb43a78 | SHARED-OBJECTS-PROFILE-01 §30, §74.1 | validations `SO-DEPTH-64`, `SO-DEPTH-65` |
| RECEIVE-LIMIT | Orchestrator decision (owner AFK), pending owner review | A client keeps a local maximum message size of at least 8 MiB. A larger value advertised in `READY` never raises its receive limit above it | LFCP-WIRE-01 §31 | none |

## Consequences

- Both SDKs gain an expansion walker that runs before every Automerge
  decode, apply and load of received or loaded bytes.
- Writers check their own changes against the same limits.
- Snapshot publishers stay within the floor.
- The walker is new security-critical parsing code. Each SDK tests it
  differentially against Automerge and fuzzes it.
- Every item awaits the owner's review; a reversal would be a later batch
  with its own baseline.
