# ADR 0007: MVP 0.1 protocol decisions, seventh batch

- **Status:** accepted (see the Status column)
- **Decided by:** the orchestrator while the project owner is away (owner
  AFK), pending owner review. The owner asked the orchestrator to proceed
  autonomously.
- **Applied by:** SPEC-PATCH-08 (baseline `mvp-0.1-baseline.8`)

## Context

While the sdk-ts expansion limits of SPEC-PATCH-07 were being implemented,
deep nesting turned out to trap the engine:
- Automerge JS 3.5.0 traps (`RuntimeError: unreachable`) applying a change
  that nests maps about 6,500 levels below the document root.
- After the trap, the wasm module is terminated for the whole process, and
  every later Automerge call fails.
- A 32 KB change of 16,384 nested maps is within the §11.1 limits, and ten
  changes of 2,000 levels trap as well: total depth is what matters.
- automerge-rs 0.12 applies, saves and loads 100,000 levels in linear time
  (Worker2's measurements), so the trap is on the JS/wasm side.
- Still, any `data/write` holder could disable Automerge in every JS
  receiver, and the §30 nesting rule decides validity only after the merge.

## Decisions

| ID | Status | Decision | Sections changed | Vectors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DOCUMENT-DEPTH | Orchestrator decision (owner AFK), pending owner review | The root is depth 0; an object (map, list, text or table) created in an object of depth `d` has depth `d + 1`, fixed at creation. No object may be deeper than 256. A receiver rejects, before its engine, a change that would create one (`PROFILE_INVALID` / `INVALID_AUTOMERGE_BYTES`), computing depths without recursion; the limit is exact because acceptance decides replica state. Writers MUST NOT exceed it. A Snapshot holds the same bound, checked from its operation columns before loading; a receiver that cannot establish the depths rejects it and falls back to the units | SHARED-OBJECTS-PROFILE-01 §11.2, §74.1 | corpus `depth`: 256 / 257, a text object at 257, a cumulative case of ten changes, a wide case, Snapshots at 256 / 257 |

## Consequences

- Both SDKs keep the depth of every object of a document and check every
  object-creating operation before their engine. Writers bound the depth
  of what they write.
- The valid Shared Objects document stays three levels below the root plus
  §30's 64.
